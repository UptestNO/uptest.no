## Key Responsibilities

- Perform non-destructive testing (VT, MT, PT, UT, PAUT) according to international standards.
- Execute industrial rope access work safely under IRATA / SOFT guidelines.
- Prepare clear inspection reports and maintain equipment.

## Requirements

- Valid NDT certifications (ISO 9712 Level 2 or equivalent).
- IRATA or SOFT rope access certification.
- Fluency in English and strong commitment to HSE standards.
- Based in Europe or North America.

- Competitive compensation and rotation schedule.

- Ongoing certification support and training.
