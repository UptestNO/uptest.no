## About the Position

We are looking for an experienced Radiography Testing (RT Level 2) operator to work on industrial and Oil & Gas offshore facilities in Norway (Bergen, Stavanger).

We primarily operate on a project-based schedule, while remaining open to permanent, long-term employment for reliable and highly qualified candidates.

## Requirements

- Hands-on experience performing Radiography Testing (RT).
- English language proficiency (minimum B2 level – for smooth communication on international sites).

## Nice to Have

- SOFT certification and/or industrial rope access experience.
- BOSIET certification.
