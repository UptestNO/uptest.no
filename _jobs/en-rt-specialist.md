## About the Position

We are looking for an experienced Radiography Testing (RT) specialist to work on industrial and offshore oil platform facilities in Norway (Bergen, Stavanger).

We primarily operate on a project-based schedule, while remaining open to permanent, long-term employment for reliable and highly qualified candidates.

## Requirements

- Hands-on experience performing Radiography Testing (RT).
- Good command of English for smooth communication on international sites.

## Nice to Have

- SOFT certification (or a higher-level IRATA certification eligible for conversion to the SOFT system) and industrial rope access experience.
- BOSIET certification.
- Knowledge of the Norwegian language.
